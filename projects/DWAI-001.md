# DWAI-001 — Sommer '90

Status: published.

[Download on itch.io](https://misterfloppy.itch.io/sommer-90).
Distribution: free download / optional donation.

## What it is

A retro-computing narrative/management game centered on the experience and
atmosphere of discovering computers in the 1990s.

## Why it is DWAI-001

Sommer '90 was developed before the DONE WITH AI framework was formally
established. It became DWAI-001 because its cross-disciplinary AI-assisted
development demonstrates the kind of building that motivated DONE WITH AI.
The identifier records its place in the experiment history; it does not imply
the framework existed when development started.

## AI-assisted workflow

The work involved game development with Godot, game systems, visual asset work,
narrative/content work, and testing and iteration with AI assistance. Historical
records do not establish a quantified split between human and AI contributions.

## What shipped

A playable game release on itch.io, available as a free download with optional
donation. The release is the published result; this repository records the
experiment without importing its source or assets.
