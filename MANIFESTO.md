# DONE WITH AI

Make things, examine what happened, and publish a useful record.

Experiments can succeed, fail, pause, or be abandoned. Keep their identities
stable so the record remains understandable. Record results and limitations
without turning missing evidence into a success story.

AI can help build and document work. Humans decide what becomes public.
Development belongs in each project's working repository; this repository
holds deliberate snapshots and records rather than a stream of development
activity.

Publish only material whose privacy, security, and redistribution rights have
been reviewed. Prefer clear explanations, useful evidence, and a small number
of meaningful changes. Avoid promotional filler and invented metrics.

Historical information that was not recorded must say "Not recorded."
Estimates must be labeled "Estimate."
